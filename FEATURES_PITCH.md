# Bhavi — Booking & Inventory System — Features Pitch

### 1. QR-Powered Mobile Camera Booking (Zero App Installs)
* **Feature Name**: QR-Powered Mobile Camera Booking
* **Its Use**: Eliminates expensive barcode scanners and messy paperwork. Salesmen do not need to download or install any app on their phones. It turns any staff smartphone into an instant booking terminal, allowing staff to register dresses in 30 seconds straight from the showroom floor.
* **How to Use It**: Staff points their normal phone camera at a QR code displayed on the shop laptop. The camera studio opens immediately in their phone browser. They snap a photo of the lehenga, type the customer’s name, phone number, and advance payment, and tap Save.

---

### 2. Studio Live Sync (Instant Screen-to-Screen Connection)
* **Feature Name**: Studio Live Sync
* **Its Use**: Connects your laptop and all staff phones in real time. When a salesman books a lehenga on the showroom floor, it instantly slides onto the owner's laptop screen at the billing counter with a notification chime. Eliminates shouting across the showroom or manual page refreshing.
* **How to Use It**: Both devices simply connect to the same shop Wi-Fi. The moment a staff member taps "Save" on their phone, the laptop dashboard automatically updates and displays the new booking card.

---

### 3. Automated WhatsApp E-Bill With Garment Photo
* **Feature Name**: Automated WhatsApp E-Bill with Photo
* **Its Use**: Replaces torn paper slips with an official luxury digital invoice. Sends the bride a branded confirmation directly to her WhatsApp featuring the actual photo of her selected lehenga, payment details, and dates. Eliminates customer disputes over what outfit was booked or how much advance was paid.
* **How to Use It**: It triggers automatically. As soon as staff saves a booking with the customer's phone number, the app formats the bill, attaches the lehenga photo, and delivers it straight to the bride's WhatsApp.

---

### 4. 3-Stage Rental Lifecycle Tracking
* **Feature Name**: 3-Stage Progress Tracker (Booked → Picked Up → Returned)
* **Its Use**: Prevents lost garments and forgotten balance payments. Gives the owner complete control over the entire journey of every single outfit so staff never hand over a dress without settling remaining payments.
* **How to Use It**: 
  - **Stage 1 (Booked)**: Automatically applied when reserved with an advance.
  - **Stage 2 (Picked Up)**: Click "Pick Up" when the bride collects her outfit; the app prompts to collect the remaining balance, clears it to ₹0 (Paid in Full), and marks the garment as taken.
  - **Stage 3 (Returned)**: Click "Return" when the bride brings the garment back after the wedding. This marks the booking complete and clears the active slot for future bookings.

---

### 5. Overdue Return Alert System ("Not Returned on Date")
* **Feature Name**: Overdue Return Alert System
* **Its Use**: Protects against double-booking disasters. If a lehenga is promised to another bride next week, but the previous bride has not returned it on schedule, you are alerted immediately so you can take action before it delays the next bride.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "⚠️ Not Returned on Date" chip on the dashboard. It instantly displays only the outfits whose return date has arrived or passed but are still marked as picked up, complete with direct phone call links to contact the customer.

---

### 6. Automated 1-Day Prior Pickup WhatsApp Reminder
* **Feature Name**: 1-Day Prior Pickup Reminder
* **Its Use**: Drastically reduces customer no-shows and saves your staff hours of manual calling. Brides get a polite WhatsApp message reminding them their outfit is ready for final trial and informing them of the exact balance amount to bring.
* **How to Use It**: Open the garment card and click "Pickup Reminder", or click the single "Send Reminders" button in the top header to dispatch all upcoming reminders at once.

---

### 7. Automated 1-Day Prior Return WhatsApp Reminder
* **Feature Name**: 1-Day Prior Return Reminder
* **Its Use**: Cuts late returns by ensuring customers return outfits on time. Sends a polite reminder 1 day before the scheduled return date with guidelines on safe return, preventing wedding delays for other clients.
* **How to Use It**: Click "Return Reminder" on any active garment card, or let the batch reminder scanner handle all due returns with one click from the header.

---

### 8. 1-Click Batch Reminders Scanner
* **Feature Name**: 1-Click Batch Reminders Scanner
* **Its Use**: Automates daily customer communication in 2 seconds. Scans the entire booking database for anyone with a pickup or return scheduled for tomorrow and messages all of them in a single batch.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "Send Reminders" button in the top header bar of your laptop dashboard every morning. The system scans all records, dispatches the WhatsApp reminders, and confirms the count on screen.

---

### 9. Matching Jewellery Add-on Tracker
* **Feature Name**: Matching Jewellery Add-on Tracker
* **Its Use**: Keeps expensive bridal jewellery sets organized and prevents inventory loss. Solves customer arguments over which accessories (choker, earrings, matha patti, nath) were included and tracks separate jewellery rental fees.
* **How to Use It**: In the booking form, check "Include Matching Jewellery", type the list of included jewellery items, and add the jewellery price. It tags the card with a jewellery badge and items are clearly printed on the customer's WhatsApp receipt.

---

### 10. Real-Time Math & Balance Due Enforcer
* **Feature Name**: Real-Time Balance Calculator
* **Its Use**: Eliminates staff math errors during busy showroom hours. Prevents revenue loss by ensuring advance payments and remaining balance amounts are calculated accurately every single time.
* **How to Use It**: Enter the Total Rental Price and the Advance Amount. The app instantly calculates and locks the Balance Due in bold text. Quick buttons (Full, Half, Reset) allow 1-tap calculations on phone screens.

---

### 11. Showroom Revenue Privacy Mode
* **Feature Name**: Financial Privacy Toggle ("Show Amount & Items")
* **Its Use**: Protects your business privacy. When customers or external visitors are standing near your computer counter, your daily collections, total revenue, and balance dues remain completely hidden from view.
* **How to Use It**: The metrics bar stays hidden by default. The owner clicks the "Show Amount & Items" eye-icon button to view Total Items, Total Rental Value, Total Cash Collected, and Total Outstanding Dues, and clicks it again to hide them.

---

### 12. Instant Multi-Field Search Bar
* **Feature Name**: Instant Multi-Field Search Engine
* **Its Use**: Finds any customer or booking in under one second. Eliminates flipping through thick paper registers when a bride calls with inquiries or visits for alterations.
* **How to Use It**: Click the search box at the top of the dashboard and start typing an Article Code (e.g., `0001`), customer name, or any part of a phone number. The matching bookings filter instantly as you type.

---

### 13. Collection Category Filters
* **Feature Name**: Collection Category Filters
* **Its Use**: Organizes your showroom inventory cleanly by outfit type so you can quickly inspect specific departments during customer trials.
* **How to Use It**: Click any collection chip on the dashboard—"Bridal", "Reception", "Sherwani", or "Casual"—to instantly isolate that category.

---

### 14. Payment Status Filters
* **Feature Name**: Payment Due / Paid Filter
* **Its Use**: Gives you instant cash-flow visibility. Allows you to see every customer who still owes money before they arrive at the boutique.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "Balance Due" chip to see only garments with pending balances, or click "Fully Paid" to see completed payments.

---

### 15. Jewellery Filter Chip ("With Jewellery")
* **Feature Name**: Jewellery Booking Filter
* **Its Use**: Helps your packing and styling staff see all outfits requiring accessory boxes in one view, ensuring no bridal jewellery set is left behind during delivery.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "💍 With Jewellery" filter chip to display only the outfits that have matching jewellery sets booked.

---

### 16. Comprehensive Date & Timeline Filter Engine
* **Feature Name**: Timeline & Event Date Filter
* **Its Use**: Enables effortless operational planning for your staff by showing exactly what needs to be packed, altered, delivered, or collected on any given day.
* **How to Use It**: Select the date type (Pickup Date, Return Date, or Booking Date), then click any timeline chip: "Today", "Tomorrow", "Next 7 Days", "This Month", "Select Month", "Specific Date", or "Date Range".

---

### 17. Desktop Manual Garment Entry ("Add Garment" Button)
* **Feature Name**: Desktop Manual Garment Entry
* **Its Use**: Allows the counter manager or shop owner to register garments and bookings directly from the laptop using existing photos saved on a computer drive or pendrive.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "+ Add Garment" button in the top right header, drag and drop the dress photo into the box, enter the customer and rental details, and click "Save Garment".

---

### 18. Sequential Article Code Engine (0001–1000)
* **Feature Name**: Sequential Article Code Engine
* **Its Use**: Standardizes garment tagging across your boutique. Automatically provides a clean, sequential 4-digit code (e.g. `0001`, `0002`) so you never accidentally tag two lehengas with the same number.
* **How to Use It**: The app automatically generates and fills in the next sequential code on every new booking. If your shop uses custom physical tags, you can simply type your custom code into the box.

---

### 19. Mobile Search & Quick Edit Mode on Phone
* **Feature Name**: Mobile Article Search & Field Edit
* **Its Use**: Allows staff walking around the showroom floor to look up any garment by its tag code, view who rented it, and update dates or fitting notes without having to walk back to the computer desk.
* **How to Use It**: In the mobile view, enter the Article ID in the top search box and tap "Find". The existing garment details and customer information load into the form ready for quick editing and updating.

---

### 20. Garment Card Quick Action Controls
* **Feature Name**: Quick Action Grid Controls
* **Its Use**: Speeds up daily counter operations by putting the most frequent actions directly on the face of every garment card, eliminating unnecessary clicks.
* **How to Use It**: On any card on the dashboard, click "Pick Up" to deliver and settle dues, click "Return" to check the outfit back into the shop, or click the green WhatsApp icon to dispatch the bill.

---

### 21. Direct Customer Calling (Click-to-Call Links)
* **Feature Name**: Click-to-Call Direct Phone Links
* **Its Use**: Saves time and eliminates dialing mistakes when contacting clients for trials, pickups, or return follow-ups.
* **How to Use It**: Tap any customer phone number displayed on a garment card or detail window from your smartphone or calling-enabled laptop to immediately dial their number.

---

### 22. Garment Detail Inspection Center
* **Feature Name**: Full Garment Detail Center
* **Its Use**: Serves as the complete command center for any single booking. Displays high-resolution photos, complete customer address, fitting notes, visual 3-stage progress, and financial histories.
* **How to Use It**: Click anywhere on a garment card. A full window opens showing customer profile details, the visual progress stepper, full payment stats, and action buttons.

---

### 23. Non-Destructive Garment Editing & Photo Swapping
* **Feature Name**: Non-Destructive Garment Editing
* **Its Use**: Protects your data integrity. Allows staff to correct typos, change dates, or upload a clearer photo without accidental data loss—leaving a field blank will never wipe existing saved customer details.
* **How to Use It**: Open the garment details, click "Edit Details", change the required fields or click the photo to replace it, and tap "Save Changes".

---

### 24. Permanent Garment Deletion ("Delete Piece")
* **Feature Name**: Permanent Garment Deletion
* **Its Use**: Keeps your showroom catalog clean by allowing the owner to permanently purge accidental duplicate entries or cancelled test bookings.
* **How to Use It**: In the garment detail window, click the red "Delete Piece" button, confirm the safety prompt, and the garment and its image are permanently removed.

---

### 25. Boutique Brand Customizer
* **Feature Name**: Boutique Brand Customizer
* **Its Use**: Personalizes the software with your store's luxury identity. Your boutique name, tagline, and logo appear across all screens and on customer WhatsApp e-bills.
* **How to Use It**: Click the pencil icon next to your store name in the top header, type your boutique name and tagline, upload your logo image, and click "Save Branding".

---

### 26. 1-Click WhatsApp E-Bill Dispatcher & Resender
* **Feature Name**: 1-Click WhatsApp E-Bill Resender
* **Its Use**: Gives you total flexibility to resend invoices or forward receipts to a bride's family member with a single click.
* **How to Use It**: Click the WhatsApp button on any card, preview the message text, change the recipient phone number if requested by the bride, and click "Send WhatsApp E-Bill Now".

---

### 27. Direct WhatsApp Gateway Integration
* **Feature Name**: Direct WhatsApp Gateway Integration
* **Its Use**: Sends WhatsApp receipts and reminders directly through your own WhatsApp number without recurring third-party cloud fees, message template approval restrictions, or manual browser popups.
* **How to Use It**: Enter your assigned WhatsApp Session ID once under the WhatsApp setup menu. Messages are dispatched directly from your shop's WhatsApp line.

---

### 28. One-Click Excel CSV Export & Local Data Safety
* **Feature Name**: One-Click Excel CSV & Database Backup
* **Its Use**: Guarantees 100% data ownership and security. Gives you an Excel-ready spreadsheet of your entire inventory and customer history for accounting or safekeeping.
* **How to Use It**: Click the "Backup" button in the top navigation bar. It instantly generates an updated `inventory_backup.csv` spreadsheet and an offline database backup snapshot inside your backups folder.

---

### 29. 100% Offline Resilience (Zero Internet Dependency)
* **Feature Name**: Offline-First Showroom Architecture
* **Its Use**: Prevents showroom paralysis during broadband or Wi-Fi outages. Your counter staff can continue booking garments, taking photos, searching records, and calculating balances locally even if the internet goes down completely.
* **How to Use It**: The system runs locally on your shop machine. When internet drops, all booking and inventory operations continue without disruption.

---

### 30. First-Time Studio Password Wizard & Security
* **Feature Name**: Studio Access & First-Run Security
* **Its Use**: Protects the application upon initial installation so unauthorized personnel cannot tamper with studio settings or configuration.
* **How to Use It**: On initial setup, enter the master studio unlock password (`open it`) to initialize the database and access the boutique management system.
